A coach builds a training session plan. The plan has approved minutes (a number the coach types, 60 by default) and a list of intervals. Each interval has a name, an effort from 1 to 10 set with a slider, with - and + buttons beside it that step to the next effort mark, and its minutes. The approved minutes are always shared across the intervals in proportion to their effort. When the coach drags one interval's minutes slider, that interval takes those minutes and the other intervals share what is left; the next change to any effort shares everything again. An interval can be locked so that it keeps its minutes. The plan can be approved only while it is valid, and approving records who approved it.
